# Limitations and known gaps

All current measurements are synthetic and generated by the bundled attack and
witness generators. The complete numeric results and evaluation scope are
maintained in [`reports/metrics.json`](./reports/metrics.json) and rendered in
[`reports/EVAL.md`](./reports/EVAL.md); this document intentionally avoids
duplicating metrics that can become stale.

## Detection and evaluation

- A closed, deterministic attack suite is not evidence of performance against
  real manifests, unknown adaptive attackers, or distribution shift.
- The holdout currently covers only the deterministic families and cases
  listed in `reports/metrics.json`. Its small sample cannot substantiate a
  general open-set precision or recall claim.
- The unknown novelty score is a fixed Jaccard distance over evidence-code
  signatures. It is not a learned or broad semantic novelty model.
- Synthetic operational witnesses are generated locally and are not signed or
  authenticated external carrier, customs, vessel, or port feeds.
- The control ledger is an assumed independent anchor, not a cryptographic
  signature. Replacing the manifest and ledger together is outside the
  detector's trust model.

## Reconstruction

- Missing row fields are recovered only when independent synthetic witnesses
  support them. Unsupported fields remain uncovered, and the row stays
  `UNRECOVERABLE`; no evaluator-side clean values are used operationally.
- Removing an anchored duplicate or fabricated record is a disposition
  decision, not payload restoration.
- Reconstruction quality measurements describe only fields attempted by the
  current witness-based methods. Consult per-attack and per-status coverage in
  the measured evaluation report.

## Streaming and deployment

- The stream is a local simulation with in-process detector and bounded recent
  event/client-queue state, SQLite event history, and WebSocket fan-out. It is
  not a durable distributed ingestion service.
- Per-event latency and throughput are not yet recorded in
  `reports/metrics.json`; Phase 5 must add measured streaming metrics before
  making performance claims.
- Out-of-order and late-event handling are not implemented.
- Authentication, authorization, multi-user controls, production secret
  management, external data integration, and high availability are outside the
  local demo's scope.
- The application is designed for loopback use. It is not hardened for public
  network exposure.

## UI and test scope

- The console demonstrates the API-backed overview, incident evidence,
  reconstruction, and live-watch workflows. It is not a production dispatch
  or case-management system.
- The E2E check exercises the built dashboard route and real local API
  endpoints. It does not cover every browser interaction, accessibility
  requirement, or production-browser environment.
